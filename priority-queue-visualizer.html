    Because a binary heap is always a complete tree, its height is always O(log n), so both sift-up and sift-down — and therefore both insert and extract — are O(log n). This is exactly the mechanics behind Heap Sort, just exposed here as a live structure you keep inserting into and extracting from, instead of a one-shot sort.
