Running commentary

Load an array to begin.

    Once a pivot lands, it never moves again — everything on its left is guaranteed smaller and everything on its right larger, forever. That's why dimmed-out regions from earlier partitions stay put while the algorithm recurses into whichever side it's currently working on.
