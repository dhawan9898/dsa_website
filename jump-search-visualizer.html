Running commentary

Load an array to begin.

    A block size of about √n balances the two phases: jumping costs at most √n probes, and the final scan inside one block costs at most √n more — O(√n) total, worse than binary search's O(log n) but with fewer, cheaper long-range jumps, which mattered more on the tape and disk drives it was designed for.
