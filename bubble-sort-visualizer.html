Bubble Sort

Running commentary

Load an array to begin.

    Every pass drags the largest remaining value to its resting place at the far end, one swap at a time — that's why the sorted region grows from the right. If a whole pass goes by with no swaps, the array was already sorted and the algorithm stops early.
