    Two extra pointers per node (prev, and a maintained tail) buy real advantages: O(1) tail insertion, O(1) deletion once you've found the node (no trailing "previous" pointer needed), and — as this visualizer shows — a choice of which end to walk from when reaching an arbitrary index.
