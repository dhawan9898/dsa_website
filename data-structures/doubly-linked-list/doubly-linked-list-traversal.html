Forward: the familiar walk

O(n)

Walking forward is identical to a singly list: start cur at the head and follow next until it's NULL. The prev pointers just come along for the ride.

 

Backward: the DLL's exclusive

O(n)

This is the one a singly list can never do. Start cur at the tail and follow prev until it's NULL. Same shape as the forward walk, mirrored — which is why a DLL is the natural fit whenever you need to move both directions (a text cursor, browser history, an undo timeline).

 

Why it's only possible here. A backward walk needs each node to know the one before it. That's exactly what the prev pointer stores — and exactly what a singly list lacks, which is why it can only ever move forward.
